Privacy policy

Bhutan Clinical Reference · Aexoryn Technologies · last updated 11 November 2026

Bhutan Clinical Reference ("the app") is a clinical reference for health workers in Bhutan, made by Dr Deo Raj Suberi and published by Aexoryn Technologies. This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. It covers the Android app on Google Play and the web app at deorajsuberibenisha.github.io/clinical-ref.

Short version

  • The clinical content (medicines, guidelines, protocols, calculators) works without sending anything about you anywhere.
  • To use the app you create an account. Your account, profile and anything you post or send to other members are stored with Google Firebase.
  • Your notes, notebooks, PDFs, settings and the patient bar stay on your phone unless you switch on note sync.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no location, and your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account and data inside the app at any time, or ask us to do it.

Kept only on your device

Settings, the patient bar (age, weight and height you type for dose calculations), recently viewed pages, pins, flashcard progress, notes (unless synced), and Eluna notebooks and imported PDFs stay on your phone or computer. Do not type patient names, CID numbers or record numbers anywhere in the app.

Services the app connects to

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores your account and community data. Google processes this data on our behalf: Firebase privacy information.
  • AI answers (optional). If you add your own Google Gemini key in Settings, your question and the matching passages from the app are sent to Google using your key. The key stays on your device.
  • Word look-up. When you look up a word, that word is sent to dictionaryapi.dev or Wiktionary.
  • Fonts and PDF tools. The app loads fonts from Google Fonts and, when you open PDFs in Eluna, the PDF and text-recognition libraries from cdnjs and jsDelivr. These services receive your device's IP address, as with any web request.
  • Links to guidelines and other websites open in your browser and are covered by those websites' own policies.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Firebase security rules limit who can read each item: private chats only by their members, profiles only by signed-in, verified members. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Messages with a disappearing timer are deleted when the timer ends.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Open profile → Delete my account (at the bottom of your profile). This deletes your sign-in, profile, the messages, photos and files you sent, your posts, your connections and requests, your synced notes and the Ludo games you hosted. You can also ask us to delete your account: see Delete your account.

A few items can only be removed by the administrator: your play-token balance, and likes and comments you left on other people's posts. We delete these within 30 days of your request.

Children

The app is for health workers and adults. It is not directed at children under 18.

Medical information

The app is a reference to support trained health workers. It does not replace clinical judgement, local protocols or the product information. AI-written answers are assistance, not verified clinical decisions.
