Delete your account

Bhutan Clinical Reference · Aexoryn Technologies

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bhutan Clinical Reference and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and tap Open profile (or tap your photo in Chat).
  3. At the bottom of your profile, tap Delete my account, then tap again to confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign in, and repeat the steps.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password) and your profile, including photo, role, workplace and registration number.
  • The messages, photos, files and voice messages you sent, and group chats you created.
  • Your For You posts, including anonymous ones.
  • Your connections and connection requests.
  • Your synced notes and the Ludo games you hosted.
  • On request by email, the administrator also removes your play-token balance and the likes and comments you left on other people's posts.

What is not affected

Notes, notebooks and PDFs that are only on your phone are not uploaded, so we cannot see or delete them; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Nothing is kept after deletion except where the law requires it.
